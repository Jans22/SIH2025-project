# Mechanical Design and Enclosure Specifications

## 1. Project Overview
This document describes the mechanical enclosure specifications for the Smart Pesticide Sprayer developed for the Smart India Hackathon (SIH) Hardware Edition 2025.
The enclosure is designed to house the relevant electronic components used in the prototype. The project also incorporates a cable-mounted carriage concept for crop monitoring and targeted spraying.

## 3. Mechanical Design

The system uses a cable-mounted carriage concept intended to move along an overhead cable for crop monitoring. The prototype design includes:
* An enclosure for electronic components.
* A carriage mechanism for movement along the cable.
* A camera mounting arrangement for crop image capture.
* A spraying assembly for liquid delivery.
